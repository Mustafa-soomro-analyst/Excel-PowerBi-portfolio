# 📦 Superstore Commercial & Fulfillment Operations Analysis (Excel)

An end-to-end retail performance and supply-chain logistics analysis evaluating 9,800 US transactions across a 4-year operational cycle (2015–2018).

---

## 🔍 Key Findings & Operational Metrics
* **Total Transactions:** 9,800 orders across 4 geographical regions (West, East, Central, South).
* **Core Demand Segment:** Consumer accounts drove 52.1% of transactional volume, with Office Supplies leading overall category frequency.
* **Fulfillment Velocity:** Average order processing duration was 3.96 days, with Standard Class representing 59.8% of aggregate freight volume.
* **Data Cleansing Scope:** Audited and resolved 2,163 unrecorded sales entries to ensure reliable metric reporting.

---

## 📊 Analytical Components
* **Data Modeling & Cleansing:** Handled missing numeric fields, extracted date dimensions, and engineered shipping cycle duration metrics (`Ship Date - Order Date`).
* **Pivot Aggregations:** Structured multi-dimensional summaries by region, shipping mode, and product line.
* **Executive Dashboard:** Built interactive controls and KPI scorecards to track seasonal demand changes and regional order fulfillment.
